Studio

Diller Scofidio + Renfro is a New York-based design studio working across architecture, visual art, and performance. Since its founding in 1981, the practice has moved from experimental installations to cultural and civic projects around the world, including the Institute of Contemporary Art in Boston, the High Line, and the transformation of Lincoln Center.

Meet Us

Ricardo Scofidio

Founding partner Ricardo Scofidio helped establish the studio’s interdisciplinary approach, bringing architecture into conversation with performance, media, and the visual arts.

Charles Renfro

Charles Renfro joined the studio in 1997 and became a partner in 2004. His work spans cultural, public, and educational projects, with a focus on how design shapes shared experiences.

Benjamin Gilmartin

Benjamin Gilmartin joined DS+R in 2004 and became a partner in 2015. He leads projects across architecture, urban design, and the public realm.
